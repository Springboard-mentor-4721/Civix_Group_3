Civic Process

How CIVIX Works

A transparent, modern platform connecting residents with local government to solve real community problems.

1

Create a Petition

Identify a critical municipal issue in your neighborhood. Set a title, description, signature goal, and target department.

2

Gather Signatures

Share with fellow residents in your jurisdiction. Verified citizens sign digitally to demonstrate collective community support.

3

Official Escalation

Once a petition reaches its signature threshold, verified city officials and municipal leaders are automatically alerted for review.

4

Action & Resolution

Track live official updates, status changes (Under Review, Victorious), and public feedback in an open, accountable timeline.

Role-Based Civic Ecosystem

CIVIX provides tailored tools for two key pillars of local democracy:

  • Citizens: Create grassroots petitions, sign community causes, vote in local polls, and track civic progress in their exact city.
  • Verified Officials: Review community concerns, provide transparent municipal status updates, and engage directly with constituents.

Smart Jurisdiction Intelligence

Civic issues are local. CIVIX features built-in high-precision location detection and real-time municipal search to ensure petitions reach the exact city, ward, or district administration responsible for taking action.

Live Community Polls & Civic Metrics

Beyond petitions, engage in real-time community ballots on urban infrastructure, environmental policies, traffic management, and municipal budgets with immediate verifiable percentage breakdowns.
