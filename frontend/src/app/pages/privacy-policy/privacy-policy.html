Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information on the CIVIX platform.

Overview

CIVIX ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our civic engagement platform.

By registering or using CIVIX, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. Last updated: January 2026.

Information We Collect

  • Full name and email address provided during registration
  • Your role (Citizen or Official) and location/jurisdiction
  • Petitions you create, sign, or interact with
  • Polls and ballots you participate in
  • Login activity, session data, and device information
  • Location data (only when you grant permission for auto-detect)

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your CIVIX account
  • To enable civic participation — petitions, polls, and official responses
  • To send email verification and password reset links
  • To enforce platform security (login limits, session management)
  • To improve platform features and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including bcrypt password hashing, JWT-based authentication, login attempt rate limiting, and session management with a maximum of 2 active devices per account.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@civix.org or visit our Contact page.
